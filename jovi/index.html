Where JOVI Began

“The shops nearby only sold logos. Not leather.”

JOVI began as a side table covered in offcuts — a cardholder cut for a friend’s birthday. Word travelled faster than the workbench could keep up, so we built a proper one. Beautiful things don’t have to be unaffordable, and neither does giving them.

We buy leather direct from tanneries and cut every order ourselves, in small batches, only once someone’s actually ordered it. No import layers, no boutique rent, no wholesale cut for a third party — what you’d pay a middleman, we put back into the leather.

Made in Malaysia — still a small workbench, not a factory.

The Material

Full-grain, not a printed layer.

Whole-hide buffalo leather, saddle-stitched by hand — two needles, one thread, locked at every pass.
