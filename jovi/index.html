The Idea

Beautiful things don’t have to be unaffordable. Neither does giving them.

JOVI started with a small complaint: everything worth gifting was either mass-produced or priced for people who don’t need to check the receipt. We wanted the middle — leather that feels considered, a name pressed into it that makes it theirs, at a price that lets you actually send it.

Why It Costs Less

We buy leather direct from tanneries and cut every order ourselves — no import layers marking up the hide before it reaches you.

Under the Stitching

Why The Bifold is worth its concept price of RM119

Full-grain buffalo leather. Sourced whole-hide, not split — so the grain you see is the grain that wears in, not a printed layer that peels.

Saddle-stitched by hand. Two needles, one thread, locked at every pass — if one stitch fails, the seam still holds.

6 card slots, 2 bill pockets. Laid out so your most-used card sits under your thumb, not buried third from the back.

Solid brass snap. No plating to flake off — brass hardware ages into a warmer tone the more it’s used.

Deboss, not print. Initials are pressed into the hide under heat, so they wear the way the leather does — never rubs off.

One box, one ribbon. No plastic window, no extra insert — just enough to make opening it feel like something.

Our Story

“The shops nearby only sold logos. Not leather.”

JOVI began as a side table covered in offcuts — a cardholder cut for a friend’s birthday. Word travelled faster than the workbench could keep up, so we built a proper one. The rule hasn’t changed since: good hide, honest stitching, and a price a friend could actually afford to send another friend.

Made in Malaysia — still a small workbench, not a factory.
