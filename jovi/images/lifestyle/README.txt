JOVI concept — lifestyle/editorial photography drop-in slots
==============================================================

  lifestyle-him.png — Men's chapter ("For Him"): a cropped, faceless
                     lifestyle shot — hand at a jacket pocket, a wallet
                     half-tucked in, torso/shoulder framing. Now live —
                     wired into index.html's .edits__figure (For Him),
                     with men-edit-fallback.svg as the onerror fallback.
  women-edit.jpg  — Women's chapter ("For Her"): a cropped, faceless
                     lifestyle shot — shoulder with a sling-bag strap,
                     hand at the bag. Square crop. Not shot yet.

Recommended: 1600 x 1600px (1:1), JPG/PNG, sRGB. Same warm paper/neutral
background tone and lighting mood as the product shots in ../products/ —
this is meant to read as the same campaign, not a different shoot.

Until a file exists at a given path, that section's onerror swaps in its
matching art-directed illustration instead (no broken image, no
placeholder card — the illustration IS the fallback here, by design).
