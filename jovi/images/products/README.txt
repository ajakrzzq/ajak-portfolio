Recommended shoot spec (keep every shot on this spec so they read as one
campaign, not six different sources):

  - 1600 x 2000px (4:5 portrait), JPG, sRGB
  - Same background tone (warm paper/cream) and camera angle across all six
  - Same lighting direction and shadow softness across all six
  - Product centred with generous headroom — CSS crops to 4:3 / 4:5 / square
    per section via object-fit: cover, so keep the product away from the
    hard edges of the frame
  - No text, no logos, no watermarks baked into the photo

These are concept-category product studies, not JOVI Leather's real product
photography. Filenames are our own concept labels (see the site's product
naming) — not JOVI Leather SKU names.
