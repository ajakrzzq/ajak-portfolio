# JOVI concept — product photography art direction

Approved shoot brief for the six Signature Products image slots (see
`README.txt` in this folder for exact filenames and dimensions). This is
for **original, generic concept product photography** — not a reproduction
of any existing JOVI Leather product, packaging, or branding.

The reference screenshots supplied during art direction are **mood/structure
references only**. Do not copy, recreate, trace, or save their branded
product imagery into this project. Nothing in the final photography may
reproduce the branding, wordmark, or recognizable product design visible in
those references.

## Master direction (applies to all six)

**Overall feel** — contemporary Malaysian accessories editorial. Affordable
premium: warm, tactile, understated, human. Produced like a small
independent leather-goods studio's campaign, not an AI-generated luxury ad.
In spirit: contemporary fashion catalogue, independent leather-goods
campaign, modern editorial product photography, tactile material
photography, sophisticated ecommerce campaign.

**Avoid** — Hermès / Louis Vuitton / Gucci pastiche, generic Shopify or
generic luxury ecommerce look, AI product-ad sheen, overly cinematic
advertising, futuristic tech branding.

**Materials** — natural full-grain leather with visible but subtle grain,
realistic stitching, natural edge finishing, subtle wrinkles and
imperfections, realistic (not plastic-looking) hardware, no excessive
gloss. The leather should read as physically touchable.

**Lighting** — soft directional daylight, warm natural highlights, gentle
side/raking light that reveals leather grain, soft contact shadows,
controlled contrast. No harsh studio flash, no rim-light glow.

**Environment** — pale textured stone, warm linen, restrained neutral/warm
palette (warm cream, espresso brown, dark chocolate, black, muted tan,
occasional restrained brass hardware). No saturated colour. Environment
stays quiet — the product is the subject.

**Camera** — high-end commercial product photography, natural perspective
and proportions, editorial 50mm feel, sophisticated shallow-but-believable
depth of field, sharp product detail, no wide-angle distortion.

**Composition** — editorial rather than generic ecommerce: intentional
negative space, products occasionally slightly off-centre, natural shadows,
simple architectural surfaces. Consistent composition language across all
six so they read as one shoot, not six different sources.

**Hard constraints, every shot** — NO brand logo. NO JOVI wordmark. NO
existing brand mark. NO text. NO watermark. NO artificial label. These are
original concept products for an independent redesign case study — believable
enough for a real contemporary accessories catalogue, without depicting any
real, identifiable brand.

## 1. The Bifold — `bifold.jpg`

A bifold leather wallet, closed, propped at a slight angle against a pale
textured stone block, resting on soft warm-toned linen. A few dried wheat
stalks softly out of focus in the background. Natural 3/4 camera angle,
moderate distance, 50mm feel. Soft raking daylight across the front face to
reveal grain and stitching. No logo, no text, no watermark.

## 2. Everyday Sling — `everyday-sling.jpg`

A compact leather sling bag with an adjustable strap, resting flat at a
slight angle on the same linen surface, strap draped naturally rather than
coiled. Soft side light catching the zip pull and hardware. Same stone and
linen environment as the rest of the set. No logo, no text, no watermark.

## 3. The Card Case — `card-case.jpg`

A slim leather card case standing upright at a slight angle on the stone
block, one card just visible at the top edge. Raking side light across the
front face to reveal grain. Shallow, believable depth of field. No logo, no
text, no watermark.

## 4. Keychain Fob — `keychain.jpg`

A small leather keychain fob with a brass ring and press stud, resting on
its side on the linen surface, close enough to read the stitching and edge
finish. Soft daylight, gentle contact shadow. No logo, no text, no
watermark.

## 5. Wallet & Card Set — `gift-set.jpg`

The bifold and the card case angled together as a paired set, the card case
resting partly atop the wallet, both sitting slightly off-centre in frame.
Same stone-and-linen surface and lighting as the rest of the set, so it
reads as part of the same shoot. No logo, no text, no watermark.

## 6. Weekender Companion — `weekender.png` (delivered)

A soft leather weekender duffel with rolled top handles, standing upright at
a slight angle, handles draped naturally. Wider shot to show full
proportions while keeping the same stone-and-linen environment and light
direction as the rest of the set. No logo, no text, no watermark.
