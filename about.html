About ajak.Web

ajak.Web is an independent website design and development service based in Petaling Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia. The goal is simple: to help businesses create a professional online presence through modern, practical and responsive websites.

A

Founder & Web Designer

Ajak

I started ajak.Web with a simple goal: to help businesses create a professional online presence through modern and practical website design. I focus on creating websites that are clean, easy to navigate and designed around the needs of each business.

Every project starts with the business, not a template — understanding what it does, who its customers are, and what will help them get in touch. I work directly with each business owner from planning through to launch, with a focus on the small and medium businesses common across Malaysia. What stays constant is the care I put into making a site that's easy for the business owner to understand and easy for their customers to use.
