Featured Case Study

Madani Sdn. Bhd.

Corporate / Oil & Gas

A professional corporate website designed to present Madani's engineering and energy capabilities with a stronger digital presence.

Madani Sdn. Bhd. — Home
Industry
Corporate / Oil & Gas
Services
Web Design, UI/UX, Frontend Development, Responsive Design
Technology
HTML, CSS, JavaScript

The Objective

Create a professional digital presence that clearly communicates Madani's engineering and energy capabilities, and presents the business with a credible corporate identity to clients, partners and candidates.

The Approach

The homepage pairs a photo of refinery infrastructure with a confident headline and two direct paths — Explore Our Services or Talk to Us — for visitors who arrive already knowing what they need. A near-navy backdrop with a single emerald accent runs consistently through the logo mark, buttons and category badges, reading as safe, technical and credible. Deeper pages extend the same structure so services, projects and industries stay easy to scan on any device.

Chicken Rice Syukran — Home
Selected Project

Chicken Rice Syukran

Food & Beverage

A warm, appetite-led website for a Hainanese chicken rice restaurant in Shah Alam, built to help hungry visitors find the menu and reserve a table without friction.

Objective

Give the restaurant a customer-friendly website that communicates its menu and story clearly, with a fast path to reserving a table.

Approach

The homepage leads with a bold headline beside a photography-ready hero panel, staged and waiting on the restaurant's own food photography, backed by two direct actions — View Menu and Reserve via WhatsApp. A cream background, brick-red accent and rounded, soft-bordered cards run through the About, Menu, Gallery, Reviews and Contact sections of the one-page site, keeping it feeling home-cooked rather than corporate.

  • Category-tabbed menu with dish descriptions and pricing
  • Contact section with an embedded map, hours and WhatsApp reservations
  • A floating WhatsApp button throughout the page

Royale Chesterfield — Home
Selected Project

Furniture Business Website

Furniture / Business

A premium showroom website for a handcrafted chesterfield sofa maker, built to present the product with the same craftsmanship it's made with.

Objective

Present a handcrafted furniture brand as established and premium, and make it easy for a visitor to book a showroom appointment.

Approach

A single continuously-scrolling page carries the visitor from collections through a custom-order journey, materials, a showroom gallery and FAQ. The hero pairs a dark charcoal-and-gold palette with a heritage stamp badge, staged on a photography-ready backdrop awaiting the brand's campaign shoot. A persistent top bar carries phone, WhatsApp and hours for both showrooms, and a single "Book a Showroom Visit" action repeats through the collections, custom-order and FAQ sections.

  • Two-step "Book a Showroom Visit" form separating personal details from visit details
  • Persistent top utility bar with phone, WhatsApp and hours for both showroom locations
  • FAQ accordion covering manufacturing, customisation, materials, delivery and warranty

Lumière Hair Studio

Beauty & Wellness

A premium modern website concept for a hair and beauty salon, designed around services, visual presentation, appointments and WhatsApp contact.

Objective

Explore how a hair and beauty salon could present its services and atmosphere online with the same polish as an in-salon experience — making it simple for a new client to see the work, understand the services on offer, and book without friction.

The concept is built around a warm, premium palette and a services grid — haircut, colour, treatment, rebonding and scalp care — presented as clearly as a printed menu, backed by a short gallery so a visitor can see the studio's actual work. Selected Project Concept Project

Safwa Umrah

Travel & Hospitality

A refined Umrah travel website concept designed to make pilgrimage planning feel clear, trustworthy and effortless. The experience focuses on package discovery, itinerary information, accommodation details and easy WhatsApp enquiries.

Objective

Explore how a premium Malaysian Umrah travel agency could present its packages, guidance and preparation resources through a calm, trustworthy digital experience — with a clear path from browsing packages to reaching out on WhatsApp.

Approach

A deep emerald and warm ivory palette, paired with a serif display face and restrained gold accents, sets a premium, spiritual tone without leaning on heavy imagery or ornamentation. A quick package finder sits above the fold, a horizontal timeline walks through the journey from Malaysia to the Holy Land, and WhatsApp enquiry buttons stay within reach throughout — all built mobile-first, since most visitors would arrive from a shared link rather than a desktop search.

  • Quick package finder that filters packages by budget, duration and travel period
  • Umrah journey timeline from preparation in Malaysia through Makkah, Madinah and return
  • Pre-filled WhatsApp enquiry links on every package for a fast, low-friction contact journey

Selected Project Concept Project

Klinik Ceria

Healthcare

A modern, warm and responsive website concept designed for a local family general clinic.

Objective

Explore how a local family clinic could turn everyday visits into a simpler experience online — clear service information, visible opening hours, and a fast way to check in with the clinic before heading over.

Approach

The concept prioritises reassurance over persuasion: a warm, uncluttered layout leads with services and hours rather than a hard sell, with WhatsApp built into the header, hours and contact sections so a visitor can ask a quick question — including whether the clinic is open right now — without picking up the phone. A calm, family-friendly palette and a short photo gallery of the clinic are designed to make a first-time visitor feel comfortable before they've even walked in.

  • WhatsApp built into the header, hours and contact sections
  • A dedicated hours section addressing "are you open now?" directly
  • Service categories covering general, family, women's, men's and preventive care
  • A short photo gallery giving a first look inside the clinic
