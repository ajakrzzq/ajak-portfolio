Restaurant Website

Restaurant / Food Business — concept for "Chicken Rice Syukran," Shah Alam

Overview

A warm, appetite-led concept for a Hainanese chicken rice restaurant. The homepage leads with a bold headline and an illustrated plate graphic, backed by two direct actions — View Menu and Reserve via WhatsApp — so a hungry visitor never has to hunt for what to do next.

Design direction

Cream background with a brick-red accent, a serif heading paired with small all-caps mono labels, and rounded, soft-bordered cards throughout — a look that reads as home-cooked rather than corporate.

Website features

  • Story-driven About page with an "at a glance" fact card
  • Category-tabbed menu with dish descriptions and pricing
  • Reviews section, gallery and social links
  • Location page with an embedded map, hours and WhatsApp reservations
  • Floating WhatsApp button on every page
  • Placeholder pricing, reviews and address are clearly flagged, ready to swap for real details before launch

Oil & Gas Corporate Website

Corporate / Oil & Gas — concept for "Madani Sdn. Bhd."

Overview

A trust-first corporate concept for an engineering firm. The hero pairs a photo of refinery infrastructure with a confident headline and two clear paths — Explore Our Services or Talk to Us — for visitors who arrive already knowing what they need.

Design direction

Near-navy backdrop with a single emerald accent used consistently across the logo mark, buttons and category badges — a palette built to read as safe, technical and credible.

Website features

  • Full navigation with a collapsible "More" menu for Sustainability, HSE and News
  • "Get in Touch" call-to-action fixed in the header
  • Filterable project case cards (Oil & Gas, Marine, Industrial, Construction)
  • Each case card tagged with category, location and year

Furniture Business Website

Furniture / Interior — concept for "Royale Chesterfield"

Overview

A premium showroom concept for a handcrafted chesterfield sofa maker established in 2010. The hero leans into heritage and craft, with a dark gold-accented banner over a leather-sofa backdrop, while the utility pages (contact, FAQ, booking) switch to clean white for readability.

Design direction

Near-black hero with a warm gold accent and a crown motif, carried through to a persistent top utility bar and a standing "Book Appointment" button — built to feel established rather than trendy.

Website features

  • Top utility bar with phone numbers per showroom, WhatsApp and hours
  • Text-size control (A / A+ / A++) for accessibility
  • "Talk to Us" contact form alongside per-showroom phone numbers
  • FAQ accordion covering manufacturing, customisation and materials
  • Two-step "Book Appointment" form separating your details from visit details
