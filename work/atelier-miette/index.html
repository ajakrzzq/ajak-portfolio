The challenge

Explore how a boutique pastry business could present its products and story through a single premium, editorial landing page — leading with strong product presentation and a fast, low-friction path to enquire.

The design direction

A single-page editorial layout pairs product photography with short brand-story copy, keeping the page focused rather than spreading the concept across a full multi-page site. A WhatsApp enquiry link sits within easy reach throughout, so a visitor who's ready to ask about an order doesn't have to hunt for a contact form.

In the detailsWhat the website does

  • 01Editorial product presentation built around photography and brand storytelling
  • 02A simple WhatsApp enquiry journey in place of a checkout or contact form
  • 03Responsive single-page layout designed mobile-first for shared links

The intentionA practical next step

A small bakery story with a strong first impression.
