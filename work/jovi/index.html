The challenge

Design a flagship e-commerce concept that avoids every "luxury ecommerce" cliché — no black-and-gold, no cold minimalism, no generic product-grid hero — while still landing the core idea: the site should feel expensive, and the products should feel like a pleasant surprise at the price.

The design direction

A warm paper-and-espresso palette with a single JOVI maroon accent, one secondary editorial colour per section rather than a full palette at once, and an Instrument Serif / Hanken Grotesk pairing chosen specifically to avoid the Fraunces-and-Inter look already used elsewhere in this portfolio. Five of the six Signature Products now carry original concept photography shot to a single consistent art direction; the sixth, plus every supporting material and construction detail, still runs on the hand-built SVG line art and colour-blocked panels the concept launched with — so the real photography sits inside the same illustrated-editorial system rather than replacing it outright.

In the detailsWhat the website does

  • 01The Gift Finder — a three-question interactive quiz (recipient, occasion, budget) that recommends curated pieces instead of a filter sidebar
  • 02Live Personalisation Preview — typed initials render instantly as an embossed monogram on the chosen product
  • 03Product Storytelling Tabs — Material, Construction, Interior, Hardware, Personalisation and Packaging, each with its own illustrated vignette
  • 04"What's in the Bag" — an editorial annotation system pairing the Everyday Sling's lifestyle photograph with numbered callouts for the pieces it's built to carry
  • 05The JOVI Wall — a rotated, mixed-size collage of customer moments and quotes styled like a physical pinboard rather than an Instagram grid

The intentionA practical next step

Leather goods, told through gifting and personalisation.
