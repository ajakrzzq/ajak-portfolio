Lumière Hair Studio

The challenge

Explore how a hair and beauty salon could present its services and atmosphere online with the same polish as an in-salon experience — making it simple for a new client to see the work, understand the services on offer, and book without friction.

The design direction

The concept is built around a warm, premium palette and a services grid — haircut, colour, treatment, rebonding and scalp care — presented as clearly as a printed menu, backed by a short gallery so a visitor can see the studio's actual work. A WhatsApp "Book Appointment" action repeats in the header, hero and a sticky mobile button, keeping booking one tap away from anywhere on the page.

In the detailsWhat the website does

  • 01Services grid covering haircut, colour, treatment, wash & blow, rebonding and scalp care
  • 02A gallery section showing the studio's work
  • 03WhatsApp booking repeated in the header, hero and a sticky mobile button

Services, atmosphere and appointments in one place.
