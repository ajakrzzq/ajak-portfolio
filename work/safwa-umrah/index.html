Safwa Umrah

A refined Umrah travel website concept designed to make pilgrimage planning feel clear, trustworthy and effortless. The experience focuses on package discovery, itinerary information, accommodation details and easy WhatsApp enquiries.

The challenge

Explore how a premium Malaysian Umrah travel agency could present its packages, guidance and preparation resources through a calm, trustworthy digital experience — with a clear path from browsing packages to reaching out on WhatsApp.

The design direction

A deep emerald and warm ivory palette, paired with a serif display face and restrained gold accents, sets a premium, spiritual tone without leaning on heavy imagery or ornamentation. A quick package finder sits above the fold, a horizontal timeline walks through the journey from Malaysia to the Holy Land, and WhatsApp enquiry buttons stay within reach throughout — all built mobile-first, since most visitors would arrive from a shared link rather than a desktop search.

In the detailsWhat the website does

  • 01Quick package finder that filters packages by budget, duration and travel period
  • 02Umrah journey timeline from preparation in Malaysia through Makkah, Madinah and return
  • 03Pre-filled WhatsApp enquiry links on every package for a fast, low-friction contact journey

The intentionA practical next step

A considered path from discovery to enquiry.
