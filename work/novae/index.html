The challenge

Show how a premium fashion retailer could present products through a clean, modern digital storefront — with easy product discovery, quick product previews and a persistent shopping bag, entirely on the front end.

The design direction

The homepage leads with an editorial hero and a serif display face, then moves into a New Arrivals grid, Shop by Category tiles and a Quick View modal for previewing colour, size and price without leaving the page. A warm neutral palette and generous whitespace keep the storefront feeling premium rather than templated, while the header collapses into a search panel and mobile menu on smaller screens.

In the detailsWhat the website does

  • 01Quick View modal for previewing colour, size and price without leaving the page
  • 02Persistent cart drawer and wishlist, saved with localStorage
  • 03Responsive navigation with a mobile menu, search panel and category browsing

The intentionA practical next step

Everyday fashion with an editorial point of view.
