Who We Are

A new generation of energy engineering, grounded in old-fashioned reliability

Madani Sdn. Bhd. was founded in Port Dickson, Negeri Sembilan, with a mandate to bring world-class engineering discipline to the region's oil & gas industry. We have since expanded our regional presence into Brunei, supporting clients across the energy value chain.

We combine technical depth with a safety-first operating culture — helping international operators, government agencies and industrial clients execute complex projects with confidence.
