Our Story

Seven years, one pot of chicken rice

Chicken Rice Syukran is a concept restaurant in Seksyen 13, Shah Alam, built around one dish: silky Hainanese chicken rice, steamed in fragrant broth and served the way it’s done across the Causeway. Over time the menu grew to include Singapore-style dishes and the Malaysian favourites regulars asked for — chicken rice stays at the centre of it all.
