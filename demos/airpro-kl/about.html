About AirPro KL

Straightforward Aircon Service for the Klang Valley

No hidden pricing, no guessing games — just a clear price, a clear explanation, and a team that comes back if something isn't right.

Why We Started

Aircon Service Shouldn't Feel Like a Black Box

AirPro KL was set up around one frustration many Klang Valley homeowners and business owners share: you call for aircon service, someone comes, and you're handed a bill — with little explanation of what was actually wrong or why it costs what it costs.

We wanted a service where the price is confirmed before a technician is ever booked, the diagnosis is explained in plain language, and the same person who assesses the problem is the one who fixes it.

That's the whole idea: no guessing games, whether you're a homeowner with one bedroom unit or a restaurant running four units through a hot kitchen.

How We Try to Work

Clarity Before Commitment

You see the price and the reasoning behind it before you agree to anything — on WhatsApp, in writing, before a technician is booked.

Respect for Your Time

We confirm an arrival window and let you know if anything changes, instead of leaving you waiting around all day.

Work We're Willing to Revisit

Every job carries a workmanship warranty — if something isn't right afterwards, we come back and make it right.

How We Try to Work On Site
