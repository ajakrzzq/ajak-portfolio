Aircon Installation

  • Indoor and outdoor unit mounting, wiring and piping
  • Vacuum test and gas charge check before handover
  • Walkthrough of the remote and basic care tips

Servicing & Chemical Cleaning

Regular servicing keeps your unit cooling efficiently and helps avoid costly repairs later. We offer general servicing and a deeper chemical wash for units that have lost cooling power.

  • Filter and coil cleaning, indoor and outdoor
  • Chemical wash for units with reduced airflow or musty smell
  • Drainage check to help prevent water leakage
  • Basic performance check once cleaning is done

05 · Refrigerant

Gas Top-Up & Leak Check

Weak cooling is often mistaken for a unit that just needs a "top-up," when there's actually a leak. We check for leaks first, so you're not paying to refill the same leak twice.

  • Leak check before any refrigerant is added
  • Refrigerant top-up to correct pressure (R32/R410A)
  • Leak point repair where accessible
  • Pressure test carried out after the top-up
