Our Story

Small Stall. Big Love for Desserts.

Mélo Pâtisserie started as a two-person kitchen with one goal: make the kind of macarons and cream puffs we'd want to eat ourselves — handmade, in small batches, with nothing rushed.

Every batch is baked and filled the same day it's sold. No freezers full of backstock, no shortcuts on ingredients — just fresh butter, real vanilla and a lot of patience.

  • Handmade in small batches
  • Fresh ingredients, sourced weekly
  • No shortcuts, no fillers
  • A small local business, made by hand
