FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer custom furniture?+

Yes. We can discuss size, configuration, fabric and finish so a piece fits your space and how you use it.

Can I choose different colours and fabrics?+

Most pieces are available in a range of fabric, leather and finish options — our team can walk you through what's available for each design.

Do you deliver across Malaysia?+

Delivery can be arranged to most areas in Malaysia. Timing and coverage depend on the piece and your location — ask our team for specifics.

Can I visit the showroom?+

Yes, walk-ins are welcome during opening hours. You're also welcome to message us on WhatsApp beforehand so we can prepare for your visit.

How long does custom furniture take?+

Lead time depends on the piece, fabric availability and configuration. Our team will confirm an estimated timeline once your order is discussed.

Do you provide interior furniture consultation?+

Yes — our showroom team can help you think through layout, scale and material choices for your room, not just a single piece.
