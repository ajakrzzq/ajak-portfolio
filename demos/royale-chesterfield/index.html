A frame built once, upholstered by hand, and made to be handed down.

Royale Chesterfield started in 2010 with a single conviction: a sofa should be built the way it used to be, before flat-pack frames and stapled cushions. Every piece that leaves our Shah Alam workshop still goes through the same kiln-dried timber frame, hand-tied springs, and deep buttoned tufting our first pieces did.

We're not the fastest furniture maker in Malaysia. We're comfortable with that.
